# QPSB Portal — Guidelines for Heads of Departments

**Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning — Controller of Examinations**

This document explains what a Head of Department (or Dean) is expected to do in the QPSB Portal, in the order the system expects it. Read Section 1 before your first login; the rest can be used as a reference.

---

## 1. Before you begin

**Access.** Open the portal link with your institutional email account signed in. The system identifies you automatically and shows only the programmes attached to your department(s). If you see an "Access Denied" screen, your email is not yet mapped in the system — write to the COE office with the email address shown on that screen.

**What you will see.** Two tabs:

| Tab | Purpose | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| 📋 **QPSB Constitution** | Nominate board members, then confirm the session date and time | Start of the QPSB cycle |
| 📁 **QPSB File Checker** | Verify that all post-QPSB files are in place, and notify the admin | After the board has met |

The portal opens on the Constitution tab by default. One card appears for each department–degree combination you are responsible for, so if you head more than one programme you will see more than one card. **Each card is handled separately** — approving one does not affect the others.

**Course list.** Every card carries a `📄 N courses ▾` chip next to the department name. Click it to expand the full list of course codes and titles that the board will cover. Verify this list is correct before you nominate anyone; if a course is missing or wrongly attached, raise it with the COE office first.

## 2. Constituting the board

### 2.1 The conflict-of-interest rule

> **Do not nominate any faculty member who has a relative or ward currently enrolled as a student in the same academic programme for which the QPSB is being constituted.**

This is the single most important check and the system cannot detect it for you. Please confirm it deliberately for each name before you tick the box.

### 2.2 Who sits on the board

- **You are the Chairperson.** Your name appears automatically in the Chairperson row. You do not select yourself and you are not counted against the member limit.
- **Nominate 2 or 3 senior faculty members** per academic programme, from the Faculty Members table below.
- The system enforces a hard ceiling of **3**. Once you have ticked three names, the remaining checkboxes grey out. Untick one to change your choice.
- The counter beside the section heading shows how many you have selected at any moment.

### 2.3 If a faculty member is missing from the list

The list is drawn from institutional records and may be filtered by the COE office (for example, to remove visiting faculty or declared conflicts). If someone you need is absent, or someone who should not be eligible is present, **do not work around it** — contact the COE office. They can add or exclude faculty for your department, and the change appears in your list immediately on reload.

### 2.4 Submitting

Click **✅ Submit QPSB Constitution**. The card status changes to **Submitted** and the nomination goes to the COE administrator for approval. You cannot edit a submitted card — this is intentional.

---

## 3. What happens after you submit

The badge at the top right of each card tells you exactly where that programme stands.

| Badge | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Not Submitted** | Board not yet constituted | Nominate and submit (Section 2) |
| **Submitted** | Awaiting COE approval | Nothing — wait |
| **Rejected** | Admin has asked for a revision | Read the reason, revise, re-submit (Section 3.1) |
| **Approved** | Constitution accepted | Wait for dates, then confirm (Section 4) |
| **Locked** | Constitution and dates both final | Nothing further on this tab |

### 3.1 If your nomination is returned

A rejected card shows a **Modifications Required** message with the administrator's reason, followed by the selection form again — with your earlier choices already ticked. Adjust what the reason asks for, leave the rest as-is, and submit again. There is no limit on re-submission.

---

## 4. Confirming the session date and time

Once the COE administrator publishes the available dates, your approved card gains a date section. Until then you will see a note saying dates will be shared shortly; there is no action for you at that point.

When the dates appear:

1. **Select dates** — tick every date on which your board can convene. You may tick more than one; multi-day boards are expected for larger programmes.
2. **Select a commencement time** — choose **one** time from the list (9:30 / 10:30 / 11:30 AM, 12:30 / 2:30 / 3:30 / 4:30 PM). This same start time applies to **all** the dates you ticked. The system does not support a different time per date.
3. Click **📅 Confirm Date & Time**.

The card then moves to **Locked** and displays the finalised dates and time. **This is not reversible from your side.** If circumstances change, ask the COE administrator to re-open date selection — they can issue a fresh set of dates, after which the card returns to you for a new choice.

A practical note: confirm the dates only after informally checking availability with your nominated members. Re-opening is possible but it costs the administrator a manual intervention and delays the schedule.

## 5. Using the File Checker (after the board has met)

Switch to the **📁 QPSB File Checker** tab. Select your programme from the dropdown and click **🔍 Check Files**. As an HoD you always run the *post-QPSB* check; the pre-QPSB check is an administrator function.

### 5.1 Reading the results

One row per course, with five status columns:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Word Track** | The scrutinised question paper in Word, with tracked changes retained |
| **Scrutinized Synopsis** | The final synopsis after board scrutiny |
| **Word Master** | The clean, accepted master copy in Word |
| **PDF Master** | The final PDF generated from the Word master |
| **Blueprint Confirmed** | Whether the blueprint certification has been completed for that course |

Green ✅ means the file is present (the count in brackets is the number of files found). Red ❌ means missing. **Every course must be green in all five columns before the work is complete.** A course showing ❌ Not Certified under Blueprint Confirmed is incomplete even if all four files are present.

The **📁 QPSB Folder** link above the table opens the Drive folder directly — use it to upload whatever is missing, then re-run the check to confirm.

**⬇ Download Full QPSB Folder** packages everything into a single ZIP for your records. Large programmes take a little time to assemble.

### 5.2 The two notification buttons

These are not interchangeable. Use the right one:

**✔ Work Done — Notify Admin**
Click this **once**, when every course shows green across all five columns for the first time. It tells the COE office the board's work is finished and ready for review. The button disables itself after a successful send, by design — it is a one-time declaration, not a status toggle.

**✔ Changes Fixed — Notify Admin**
This stays greyed out unless the administrator has reviewed your files and sent back a "Changes Required" notice. When that happens, the button becomes active. Make the corrections in the Drive folder, re-run the file check to confirm everything is green again, and only then click it. Clicking before you have actually fixed the files sends a false all-clear and will simply come back to you.

## 6. Sequence at a glance

```
Verify course list  →  Nominate 2–3 faculty  →  Submit
                                                  ↓
                                    (returned?) → revise → re-submit
                                                  ↓
                                              Approved
                                                  ↓
                              Admin publishes available dates
                                                  ↓
                          Tick date(s) + one start time → Confirm
                                                  ↓
                                              Locked
                                                  ↓
                                    ——— board convenes ———
                                                  ↓
                         File Checker → all five columns green
                                                  ↓
                                    "Work Done — Notify Admin"
                                                  ↓
                          (changes requested?) → fix → re-check
                                                  → "Changes Fixed — Notify Admin"
```

## 7. Common questions

**I head two programmes. Can I nominate the same faculty for both?**
Yes — each card is independent, and the same person may serve on more than one board provided the conflict-of-interest rule holds for each programme separately.

**Can I withdraw a submission?**
Not directly. Ask the COE administrator to return it; you will then get the revision form with your original selections intact.

**A member has to drop out after the board is approved.**
Contact the COE office. The administrator can add a member to an approved or locked board without resetting the whole constitution.

**The page seems stuck on "Loading…" or an action does nothing.**
Refresh the page and retry. If it persists, note the message shown and report it to the COE office — server-side errors are logged and can be traced.

**Do I need to do anything on the Constitution tab after Locked?**
No. That programme's constitution phase is complete; everything further happens in the File Checker.

## Editor's note — differences from the current build

This guide is the institution's canonical operating document and is kept
here verbatim. The in-progress React rewrite of this portal (this repository)
does not yet implement every detail described above. As of this writing:

- The final status badge in this build reads **"Scheduled"**, not "Locked"
  (the underlying board status is still internally named `Locked`).
- There is no separate "🔍 Check Files" button — the check runs automatically
  once a programme is selected in the File Checker.
- The check matrix currently has **four** columns (Word Track, Scrutinised
  Synopsis, Word Master, PDF Master). The **Blueprint Confirmed** column
  described in §5.1 is not yet implemented.
- **"⬇ Download Full QPSB Folder"** is not yet implemented in this build.

The in-app FAQ widget (`src/shared/constants/faq.ts`) is derived from this
guide but describes the software as it actually behaves today — see that
file's comments for exactly where it departs from this document.
